`pttrn-checkbox`: the Checkbox as a form-associated Web Component, with `indeterminate`, `invalid` and `required` (which blocks submission while unchecked). It submits `value` under `name` while checked, resets with its form, is named by aria-label or a `<label>`, and fires `pttrn-change` and `change`.
