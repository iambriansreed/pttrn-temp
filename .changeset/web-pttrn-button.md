`pttrn-button`: the Button as a Web Component. Label (attribute or slotted text), `icon` slot and `icon-only`, the three variants, four sizes, `destructive`, `width="fill"`, a tooltip that names an icon-only button, `href` for a link with role button, and `type="submit"` or `"reset"` acting on its form.
